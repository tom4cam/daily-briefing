Daily Social Briefing

Your personal daily briefing

This wizard builds a personalized AI prompt that scans social media, news, and the web each morning and delivers a summary of what matters to you: family activity, friends, work topics, and industry news, along with suggested replies and ready-to-post drafts.

It takes about two minutes to set up. At the end you will get a prompt you can copy and use in two ways:

Option 1: Automated daily delivery

Use Claude Cowork (free desktop app at claude.ai/download) to schedule the prompt to run every morning. Set your delivery destination (Slack, email, or Google Doc) and it arrives automatically.

Option 2: Run it manually anytime

Open Claude.ai (free account works), turn on web search in the chat, paste your prompt, and hit send. No app or setup needed.

Nothing you enter here is stored or sent anywhere. The wizard runs entirely in your browser and only produces a text prompt for you to copy.
